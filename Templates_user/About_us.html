About DCRS

Digital Crime Reporting System is a web-based project designed to provide citizens with a convenient platform to submit crime complaints, access complaint-related information and learn about safety and legal awareness.

📘 Introduction

The Digital Crime Reporting System (DCRS) is a student project that aims to simplify the process of recording and managing crime-related complaints through a digital platform.

Users can register or log in, submit complaint details, provide supporting information and check the available status of their complaints. The system is designed with a simple interface to make its main features easier to understand and use.

🎯 Our Purpose

The main purpose of DCRS is to demonstrate how web technology can help organize complaint information and make complaint tracking more convenient for citizens.

⚙️ How DCRS Works

1

Create an Account or Log In

The user accesses the website through the registration or login page.

2

Submit a Complaint

The user fills in the available form with incident details and submits it.

3

Store Complaint Information

The application processes the submitted information and stores it in the configured database.

4

Check Complaint Status

The user opens the complaint status page to view the status information available in the system.

🚀 Project Goal

Our goal is to develop an understandable and user-friendly demonstration of a digital complaint management platform. The project also provides an opportunity to learn frontend development, backend programming, database management and application design.

⚠️ Important Notice

DCRS is an educational project and is not an official government or police website unless formally authorized. Submitting a complaint through this demonstration does not guarantee that it has reached the police or any government authority. In an actual emergency, contact the appropriate official emergency service directly.
